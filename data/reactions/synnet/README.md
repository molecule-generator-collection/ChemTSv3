## Hartenfeller–Button reaction templates

Source (MIT License):
https://github.com/wenhao-gao/SynNet/blob/05bc5646d23ba716969373f326440548d271e96c/data/assets/reaction-templates/hb.txt

This 91-template collection was assembled by the SynNet authors from
publicly available template sets reported by Hartenfeller et al. (2011)
and Button et al. (2019).
